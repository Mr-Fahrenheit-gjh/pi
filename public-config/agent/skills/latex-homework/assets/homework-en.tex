\problem{1}{Rates and inflation}
\heading{(a) APR and effective annual rate}
The six-month holding-period return is $r_{6m}=0.032$. With two periods per year,
\begin{align*}
\mathrm{APR}&=2r_{6m}=\boxed{6.4\%},\\
\mathrm{EAR}&=(1+r_{6m})^2-1=\boxed{6.5024\%}.
\end{align*}
